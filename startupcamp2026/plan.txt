Topic 04 (ТИСБИ catalogue): «Дневник выполнения назначений врача»
Saved: 2026-10-08

1. RESEARCH (web, sources kept for the last slide)
- Problem size: adherence data for Russia and globally (WHO, Russian clinical
  publications), chronic-disease prevalence, polypharmacy in older patients.
- Market: Russian digital health / mHealth market size, pharmacy market and
  e-pharmacy share (DSM Group, AlphaRM, RNC Pharma), smartphone penetration
  by age group.
- Competitors: Russian-available reminder apps (MyTherapy, Medisafe and local
  ones), pharmacy ecosystems (Аптека.ру, ЕАПТЕКА, Здравсити, Ютека), health
  platforms (СберЗдоровье, Госуслуги/ЕМИАС), plus the "do nothing"
  alternatives: alarm clock, pill box, paper.
- Russian specifics:
  * 152-ФЗ: health data is a special category and must be stored in Russia.
  * Advertising law: prescription-drug ads to consumers are banned, and online
    ads need labelling (erid) - this directly limits the banner idea.
  * Medical-device rules: when software has to be registered with
    Росздравнадзор, and how to stay outside that.
  * Distribution and payments: RuStore, СБП, limits on App Store / Google Play
    billing.
  * Online sale of medicines: what is and isn't allowed for prescription drugs.
